## Curated findings (close reading of first-party talks and docs)
- **Retention is loyalty, so protect trust.** Long-lived games stayed true to their vision. Chasing every trend makes a game "top-heavy" until it "caves in on itself". — Onett & Lauren Freeman (Roblox), [RDC 2025 Retention Playbook @ 17:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=1030s), [@ 28:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=1703s)
- **Pace content rather than dumping it.** Ten guns in one week reads the same as two; ship one a week for ten weeks. A season pass releases a lot at once but unlocks it progressively. — Colin (Gunfight Arena) & Alex (Jailbreak), [@ 14:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=848s), [@ 15:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=920s)
- **Ship with content held back and use the real-world calendar.** Launch with room for updates. Holidays give a default live-ops theme for weeks you are short of ideas. — Colin (Gunfight Arena), [@ 12:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=776s)
- **Pick updates at the overlap of what players want and what the team wants to build.** Jailbreak settled on weekly updates; Adopt Me ships every Friday, 52 weeks a year, and plans each update's monetisation six weeks ahead. — [@ 10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=615s); Jesse (Adopt Me), [RDC 2025 Mastering Monetization @ 30:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=1817s)
- **Watch the first 48 hours of every update** for misuse and unfair advantages. — Henry (Evade), [@ 20:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=1233s)
- **Watch the silent majority, not the loudest voices.** "For every player that comes to you there are hundreds" who never comment. Watch what players do, in-game and on YouTube. — Colin (Gunfight Arena), [@ 35:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=2132s)
- **Personalise rewards to the player's current friction point** (gems for the payer who needs gems), and show tomorrow's reward to create intent. — Leon Fernandes, [Gamesforum @ 4:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=61BjLzI490g&t=253s)
- **Make sessions social.** Roblox recommendation ranking reads co-play alongside engagement, return visits and spend. — [Roblox Creators, Recommended for You @ 0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K7jsk3bzmvE&t=40s)
- **Measure by cohort.** Retention dashboards cohort new users by first-play date (D1/D7/D30), and Journeys show branching drop-off paths. In Roblox's example, 64% of users skip the shop and it has the highest churn (10%); power-ups are skipped by 82% yet have 0% churn. — [Docs: Retention](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention), [DevForum: Journeys](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-understand-complex-player-funnels-with-journeys/4973876)
- **Risk flag:** creator advice treating FOMO as "the ultimate retention weapon" ([AlvinBlox @ 6:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y93DRfB-Deo&t=413s)) conflicts with Roblox's own guidance to make monetisation "fair and transparent" ([DevForum, Chief Growth Officer](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042)). Use time-limited content with clear end dates and earnable paths, never fake urgency.

## Roblox implementation playbook
- **Retention KPIs & churn**: Track D1/D7/D30 by new-user cohort in the Retention dashboard; annotate every update date. Pair retention with playtime and funnel data to locate where players quit.
  - APIs: Creator Hub Analytics: Retention, Engagement, AnalyticsService custom events
  - Measure: D1, D7, D30 retention; Average playtime per DAU; First-play bounce rate
- **Social play & friends**: Reward playing with friends (shared bonuses, co-op objectives) and make inviting one tap. Give players something to show off to others in the same server.
  - APIs: SocialService:PromptGameInvite, Players:GetFriendsAsync, TextChatService
  - Measure: % sessions with a friend; Invites sent per DAU; Retention of social vs solo players
- **Quests, tasks & achievements**: Rotate daily and weekly quests that point at the core loop and at under-used features. Use badges for one-time milestones that are visible on the profile.
  - APIs: BadgeService, DataStoreService for quest state, AnalyticsService:LogFunnelStepEvent for quest chains
  - Measure: Quest completion rate; Feature adoption after quest exposure
- **Competition & leaderboards**: Combine an all-time leaderboard with seasonal or daily boards so new players can place. Show rank deltas and the next rival above the player.
  - APIs: OrderedDataStore (all-time), MemoryStoreService sorted maps (fast/seasonal boards)
  - Measure: Leaderboard view rate; Session length of competitive players
- **Scarcity & FOMO**: Use limited-time content to create reasons to return, with clear end dates and earnable paths. Rotate items back later so scarcity drives timing, not exclusion.
  - Measure: Return rate during limited windows
- **Daily rewards & streaks**: Track lastClaimDay and streak server-side in UTC days (os.time() // 86400); never trust client time. Escalate rewards across a 7-day cycle with a highlight on day 7; show tomorrow's reward to create intent. Use a forgiving streak (grace day or partial reset) so one missed day does not erase the habit.
  - APIs: DataStoreService:UpdateAsync (atomic claim), ExperienceNotificationService:PromptOptIn for 'reward ready' reminders
  - Measure: D1 / D7 retention; Daily claim rate; Streak length distribution
