## Curated findings
### Onboarding
- **The FTUE is "arguably the single most important piece of content in a free-to-play game."** Keep it to a few minutes, and expect to keep iterating after launch. D1 retention measures it. — [Roblox Creators, Onboarding Essentials @ 0:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XfxbdKtSbOI&t=51s), [@ 1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XfxbdKtSbOI&t=99s)
- **Teach only the essentials:** movement and interaction, then the core loop (what to do *and why*). Surface unusual controls on the HUD when they are relevant. Layer complexity after the first session. — [@ 3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XfxbdKtSbOI&t=226s), [@ 4:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XfxbdKtSbOI&t=267s), [@ 5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XfxbdKtSbOI&t=309s)
- **Front-load progress.** Tune the level curve so players level up quickly in the first minutes, give starter items and some currency so they visit the shop, and make onboarding social, never isolated. — [@ 5:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XfxbdKtSbOI&t=344s), [@ 7:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XfxbdKtSbOI&t=427s)
- **End the first session with aspiration.** Show the full range of what is coming (mid- and long-term goals visible in a hub or on the HUD, not buried in menus). Close with a congratulatory call to action, ideally unlocking a new ability. — [@ 8:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XfxbdKtSbOI&t=501s), [@ 9:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XfxbdKtSbOI&t=576s), [@ 10:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XfxbdKtSbOI&t=614s)
- **Three guidance techniques:** visual elements (a subtle trail, or a highlighted UI element), contextual "just-in-time" tutorials triggered by play (e.g. when a player is two-thirds of the way to unlocking a feature), and timed hints shown only to players who take longer than most. — [Roblox Creators, Onboarding techniques @ 0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXqZC4x2Vn8&t=38s), [@ 1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXqZC4x2Vn8&t=74s), [@ 1:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dXqZC4x2Vn8&t=117s)
- **Protect the FTUE as the game grows.** "Don't mess with the first-time user experience." When adding seasons, make the first new items fun but balanced against starter gear. Test with genuinely new players, because veteran testers become entrenched. — Colin & Henry, [RDC 2025 Retention Playbook @ 31:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=1884s), [@ 30:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=1839s)
- **UI is communication.** Prioritise what players need *now* (use contextual buttons), steer attention with colour, size and space, keep the palette small and button styles consistent, follow conventions (red X closes; E to interact), and pick legible fonts. — [Roblox Creators, UI/UX Essentials @ 1:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lOOvOIJoMi4&t=98s), [@ 3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lOOvOIJoMi4&t=198s), [@ 12:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lOOvOIJoMi4&t=731s)
- **Design UX for the real audience.** Very young players have less manual dexterity. Write user stories, draw flow diagrams, and remove pain points; for example, let players buy consumables in multiples instead of round-tripping to the shop. — [@ 10:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lOOvOIJoMi4&t=653s), [@ 15:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lOOvOIJoMi4&t=905s)
- **Measure, then segment.** Experiments can now target new players and break results down by segment (e.g. did the new onboarding help U18 more than 18+?). Difficulty can differ for new players without separate configs. — [DevForum: Evolving Experiments and Analytics](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/evolving-experiments-and-analytics-segmentation-experiment-targeting-early-access-program-and-more/4828462)

### Retention
- **Retention is loyalty, so protect trust.** Long-lived games stayed true to their vision. Chasing every trend makes a game "top-heavy" until it "caves in on itself". — Onett & Lauren Freeman (Roblox), [RDC 2025 Retention Playbook @ 17:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=1030s), [@ 28:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=1703s)
- **Pace content rather than dumping it.** Ten guns in one week reads the same as two; ship one a week for ten weeks. A season pass releases a lot at once but unlocks it progressively. — Colin (Gunfight Arena) & Alex (Jailbreak), [@ 14:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=848s), [@ 15:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=920s)
- **Ship with content held back and use the real-world calendar.** Launch with room for updates. Holidays give a default live-ops theme for weeks you are short of ideas. — Colin (Gunfight Arena), [@ 12:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=776s)
- **Pick updates at the overlap of what players want and what the team wants to build.** Jailbreak settled on weekly updates; Adopt Me ships every Friday, 52 weeks a year, and plans each update's monetisation six weeks ahead. — [@ 10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=615s); Jesse (Adopt Me), [RDC 2025 Mastering Monetization @ 30:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=1817s)
- **Watch the first 48 hours of every update** for misuse and unfair advantages. — Henry (Evade), [@ 20:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=1233s)
- **Watch the silent majority, not the loudest voices.** "For every player that comes to you there are hundreds" who never comment. Watch what players do, in-game and on YouTube. — Colin (Gunfight Arena), [@ 35:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgyfcMfxQcI&t=2132s)
- **Personalise rewards to the player's current friction point** (gems for the payer who needs gems), and show tomorrow's reward to create intent. — Leon Fernandes, [Gamesforum @ 4:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=61BjLzI490g&t=253s)
- **Make sessions social.** Roblox recommendation ranking reads co-play alongside engagement, return visits and spend. — [Roblox Creators, Recommended for You @ 0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K7jsk3bzmvE&t=40s)
- **Measure by cohort.** Retention dashboards cohort new users by first-play date (D1/D7/D30), and Journeys show branching drop-off paths. In Roblox's example, 64% of users skip the shop and it has the highest churn (10%); power-ups are skipped by 82% yet have 0% churn. — [Docs: Retention](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/analytics/retention), [DevForum: Journeys](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/analytics-understand-complex-player-funnels-with-journeys/4973876)
- **Risk flag:** creator advice treating FOMO as "the ultimate retention weapon" ([AlvinBlox @ 6:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y93DRfB-Deo&t=413s)) conflicts with Roblox's own guidance to make monetisation "fair and transparent" ([DevForum, Chief Growth Officer](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/boost-your-discovery-by-building-games-people-want-to-play/4779042)). Use time-limited content with clear end dates and earnable paths, never fake urgency.

### Monetization
- **"Monetization is engagement."** Ten players spending 100 Robux beats one spending 1,000 for longevity and for discovery, so get new players to a first purchase fast. — Jesse (Adopt Me), [RDC 2025 Mastering Monetization @ 22:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=1357s)
- **Personalise by player stage.** New players get a cheap starter pack; casual players an item that deepens engagement; high spenders exclusive, limited offers. SpongeBob Tower Defense runs six escalating starter bundles from a 25–99 Robux entry point, then elite bundles. — Roblox, [@ 3:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=197s); Zach (SpongeBob Tower Defense), [@ 21:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=1283s)
- **Sell speed and convenience, not victory.** "Extremely free to play, but it's pay to speed." A free taste (2x speed for joining the group) converted a 3x product. Never gatekeep basics like movement. — Zach, [@ 20:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=1252s), [@ 25:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=1502s)
- **Monetise every intended mechanic at launch.** Adding a paid tier later feels like taking something away, and late monetisation draws "here comes pay-to-win" backlash. Avoid high-priced offers to brand-new players. — Zach, [@ 23:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=1391s), [@ 30:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=1848s)
- **Make spending plannable.** Adopt Me uses a templatised cadence, e.g. a known 1,000-Robux legendary pet each event week, plus value-adds (fly/ride potions) for engaged players. — Jesse, [@ 14:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=861s)
- **Rewarded video ads reach the 80%+ of players who never pay.** Place them in high-traffic areas (lobby, shop) and natural breaks (between levels), teach players about them, and make the reward meaningful. SpongeBob TD gives tiered soft currency plus a tradable cosmetic after 10 ads, and older, superseded passes as ad milestones, reporting 90%+ fill. — Roblox, [@ 7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=465s), [@ 9:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=557s); Zach, [@ 15:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=934s), [@ 18:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=1087s)
- **Let the platform price.** Price Optimization finds prices "at no engagement loss", and Regional Pricing (5,000+ experiences) raised monetisation *and* engagement. Item titles and descriptions now feed personalised item ranking, so write them well. — Roblox, [@ 4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=241s), [@ 4:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=284s), [@ 5:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=322s)
- **Prefer consumables for repeatable value.** Turning would-be consumables (e.g. 2x offline earnings) into game passes caps revenue. Best sellers remove a pain players already feel. — MoseyTube77, [@ 4:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-PoyiiGhoNo&t=295s), [@ 3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-PoyiiGhoNo&t=185s). Place developer products where foot traffic is, and judge them on playtime as well as Robux. — Tizzy RBLX, [@ 11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zoz3PSMqets&t=673s), [@ 14:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zoz3PSMqets&t=878s)
- **Genre sets the ceiling.** One studio estimates ARPPU around $2 in hyper-casual "brainrot" games versus around $20 in tower defense with deeper mechanics. Selling exclusive units can hurt one and strengthen the other's trade economy. — Zach, [@ 29:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJ-zYfL10Ew&t=1751s)
- **Platform mechanics to build against (first-party):** grant developer products in `ProcessReceipt` (Listed products sold outside the game need it implemented correctly); personalise the shop with `RankProductsAsync` / `RecommendTopProductsAsync`; handle the Transaction Refunded webhook; developer subscriptions in Robux (with promotional pricing) were announced at RDC 2025 for 2026. Engagement-Based Payouts were **deprecated on 2025-07-24** (replaced by Creator Rewards). — [Docs: Developer Products](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/developer-products), [DevForum: Refund webhook](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/new-transaction-refunded-webhook-notifies-when-a-purchase-is-cancelled/4890339), [Docs: Engagement-based payouts](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/engagement-based-payouts)
